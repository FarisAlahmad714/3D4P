Posting Guidelines
Donation Request Guidelines
  • Provide clear, honest details about your prosthetic needs
  • Include medical documentation if available
  • Set realistic funding goals
  • Upload clear photos when appropriate
  • Respond to donor questions promptly
  • Provide updates on your progress
Community Post Guidelines
  • Be respectful and supportive to all community members
  • Share authentic experiences and stories
  • Avoid promotional or commercial content
  • Use appropriate language and imagery
  • Engage constructively with comments
  • Report inappropriate content when necessary
Safety & Privacy
  • Never share personal information like addresses or phone numbers
  • Be cautious about sharing identifying details
  • Report suspicious or inappropriate behavior
  • All posts are moderated for safety and appropriateness
