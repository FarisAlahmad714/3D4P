How It Works

Learn how our platform connects those in need with generous donors worldwide

1

Create Account

Sign up for free and provide basic information. No payment required to get started.

2

Submit Request

Create a detailed request explaining your prosthetic needs and upload supporting documentation.

3

Get Verified

Our team reviews your request within 24-48 hours to ensure authenticity and legitimacy.

4

Go Live

Once approved, your request goes live for donors to see and support worldwide.

5

Receive Donations

Generous donors contribute to your prosthetic fund. Track progress in real-time.

6

Get Your Prosthetic

Once funded, receive assistance in obtaining your prosthetic device and regain your mobility.
